Overview

Travel trailer and RV owners renting across multiple platforms—such as RVezy, RVShare, and Outdoorsy—faced a fragmented, labor-intensive workflow. Managing listings, keeping calendar availability synchronized, and responding to prospective renters required logging into three separate dashboards, manually updating dates to prevent costly double bookings, and tracking revenue across disconnected spreadsheets. Furthermore, platforms intentionally obscured renter contact info, forcing owners to maintain disjointed communication channels.

Rental Reactor was conceptualized, designed, and deployed from the ground up to solve this operational overhead. Featuring a centralized fleet dashboard, unified multi-platform calendar, consolidated messaging inbox, and an AI-powered communications engine, Rental Reactor brings multi-platform rental operations under a single pane of glass. Rather than following a conventional, drawn-out development lifecycle, Rental Reactor was delivered through agentic AI workflows—leveraging Claude Code, Google Antigravity, and Figma Dev integrations to collapse the 0-to-1 product delivery timeline from months into weeks as a solo product designer and engineering operator.

The Problem

RV owners operating independent rental fleets face distinct logistical hurdles that general hospitality tools do not address:

  • Cross-Platform Synchronization: Calendar sync delays between RVShare, Outdoorsy, and RVezy frequently result in conflicting reservations, cancellation penalties, and hurt platform search rankings.
  • Fragmented Communication: Messages arrived via email, SMS, and siloed in-app platform inboxes with varying notification delays, making rapid response times difficult to maintain.
  • Obscured Guest Data: Platform policies restrict early access to customer phone numbers and direct emails, impeding off-platform customer relationship building and upselling.
  • Scattered Financial Tracking: Calculating true net margins required manual reconciliation of differing platform service fees, insurance withholdings, and cleaning deposits.

Process & Agentic Engineering

As both the Principal Product Designer and solo builder, I established an accelerated delivery loop powered by modern AI agentic tooling:

Figma Design System & Token Architecture: Built a component-driven design system in Figma tailored for high-density SaaS dashboards. Established strict semantic tokens for color, typography, spacing, and component states that directly mapped to Tailwind CSS utilities.

Context-Engineered Handoffs via Figma Dev Integrations: Using Figma Dev integrations and structured component metadata, I bypassed ambiguous design specifications. UI components, responsive layout constraints, and interaction states were fed directly into agentic coding environments with high semantic fidelity.

Autonomous Code Generation with Claude Code & Antigravity: I leveraged Claude Code and Google Antigravity to execute full-stack implementation. By defining modular architectural contracts, database schemas, and prompt architectures, coding agents autonomously scaffolded React components, backend scraping workers, and API routes. This eliminated the traditional friction and latency of engineering handoffs.

Human-in-the-Loop Iteration: Maintained rigorous quality control by pairing rapid UX wireframes with agentic test harness generation. Because feature implementations were completed in hours rather than sprints, I could rapidly prototype, test edge cases with real platform payloads, and refine UX micro-interactions in real time.

Solution

Rental Reactor delivers an end-to-end command center for multi-platform RV rental operators:

  • Fleet Command Dashboard: High-level real-time visibility into active bookings, monthly occupancy rates, projected revenue, pending guest inquiries, and fleet maintenance status.
  • Unified Master Calendar: Color-coded aggregate calendar displaying real-time reservations, platform origins, turnaround buffers, and maintenance blackout windows across all RV units.
  • Consolidated Messaging & AI Co-Pilot: A single inbox uniting guest inquiries across RVezy, RVShare, and Outdoorsy with booking context badges. Includes an approval-based AI draft response engine with a quick-edit feedback loop to ensure replies match the owner's voice before dispatch.
  • Fleet Listing Management: Granular unit profiles detailing equipment specifications, platform-specific pricing rules, platform credential vaults, and automated scraper telemetry indicators (green/yellow/red) to monitor third-party sync health.

Outcome & Impact

By pairing deep product design principles with agentic software execution, Rental Reactor achieved milestones typical of a full product team:

  • Drastic Velocity Acceleration: Collapsed the 0-to-1 development timeline from an estimated 4–6 months down to weeks, enabling rapid validation of core SaaS assumptions.
  • Clean System Maintainability: Established reusable prompt architectures and component patterns that allow the codebase to scale cleanly without architectural debt.
  • Targeted Beta Rollout: Beta deployment scheduled for early 2026 with a dedicated cohort of multi-unit RV fleet owners recruited through initial user research interviews.

Personal Learning & Insights

Rental Reactor solidified my perspective on the future of software delivery: the most effective product builders of tomorrow will be Design Technologists who leverage Agentic AI to turn strategy into shippable production code directly. Designing with Claude Code and Antigravity demonstrated how prompt architecture and context engineering serve as the new connective tissue between design tokens and frontend implementation, allowing a solo operator to execute at the speed of thought without sacrificing user-centered design standards.
